A place for possibility

What isMogshade?

The space between the thing that is and the thing it might become.

Mogshade happens when people make meaning from stories, symbols, play, pictures, objects, memories and jokes. It is the potential waiting inside ordinary things.

Our games make room for tables full of friends, families finding adventures together, and quiet evenings spent wandering through your own imagination.
