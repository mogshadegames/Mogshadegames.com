Mogshade Games

Mogshade Games is a storytelling and community-led publisher creating tabletop games, adventures, journals, tools, and strange little doorways into imagination.

We make games for people who want to gather, wonder, laugh, think, build, explore, and make meaning together. Some of our stories are shared around a table with friends. Some are played with family. Some are written quietly by candlelight, in the hush between one thought and the next.

At the heart of everything we make is the belief that stories help people connect: with each other, with themselves, and with the worlds they might dare to imagine.

What is Mogshade Games?

Mogshade Games is a place for stories.

It is for evenings around a table with snacks, laughter, friends, and a problem everyone wants to solve together. It is for families opening a door into adventure. It is for people writing alone, wandering through their own thoughts, and finding something waiting there.

We create games and creative resources that help people play, reflect, imagine, and connect. Our work ranges from child-led doorway adventures to strange mythic games, dystopian dungeon crawls, journaling tools, and other story-shaped things still rustling in the trees.

What is Mogshade?

Mogshade is the space between the thing that is and the thing it might become.

It is the hush between the trees. The weight that comes through the branches when the ordinary world begins to listen. It is what happens when people gather around symbols, jokes, warnings, maps, dice, pictures, questions, memories, and half-made ideas, then turn them into meaning.

Mogshade is not one world. It is the feeling before a story opens.

Who is Mogshade Games for?

Mogshade Games is for you.

If you have travelled far enough to ask the question, then you are already near the door.

It is for people who want entertainment, wonder, laughter, danger, reflection, and strange beautiful nonsense. It is for people who want to sit around a table with snacks, laughter, and friends, look at a puzzle or problem, and make it everyone's entertainment for the evening: everyone's game, everyone's enjoyment.

It is for families making core memories. It is for readers, writers, drawers, makers, builders, troublemakers, and quiet wanderers. It is for people who want to be with others, and people who want to spend better time with themselves.

What we make

We make storytelling games and creative resources that invite people to play, imagine, and take part.

Some are full tabletop roleplaying games. Some are smaller adventures, journals, prompts, tools, printable aids, or strange objects for the table. Some are made for family play. Some are darker, weirder, sharper, or more grown-up. Some are built to be read, some to be played, and some to sit with you until you decide what they mean.

Across all of them, our aim is the same: to help people open the door and see what is waiting.

Our publishing approach

Mogshade Games is digital first, shaped with the community, and printed when there is demand.

We want to build carefully, openly, and sustainably. That means sharing work, listening to the people who gather around it, supporting games after release, and creating physical products when the audience is there to hold them.

The story comes first. The community shapes the path. The printed thing arrives when the door is ready to stay open.
