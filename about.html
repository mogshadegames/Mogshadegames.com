About Mogshade Games

The space between the thing that is and the thing it might become.

A warm, strange place for gathering, wandering, wondering and making meaning through play.

01 · The workshop

Mogshade Games

Mogshade Games is a storytelling-led, community-minded publisher creating tabletop games, adventures, journals, tools and strange little doorways into imagination.

We make games for people who want to gather, wonder, laugh, think, build, explore and make meaning together. Some of our stories are shared around a table with friends. Some are played with family. Some are written quietly in the space between one thought and the next.

At the heart of everything we make is the belief that stories help people connect with each other, with themselves, and with the worlds they might dare to imagine.

02 · The meaning

What is Mogshade?

Mogshade is what happens when people gather and make meaning from the world around them.

It appears in stories, symbols, play, pictures, objects, memories, warnings, jokes and shared structures. It is the potential of what something can be, discovered through the things that already are.

It is the hush between the trees and the weight that comes through the branches when the ordinary world begins to listen. It is the feeling before a story opens.

03 · The invitation

Who is it for?

Mogshade Games is for you.

If you have travelled far enough or read enough to reach the question, you are already near the door.

Perhaps you want candlelit journaling, something fun to read, draw or write, family play that becomes a core memory, or an evening around a table with snacks, laughter and friends. You might look at a puzzle or problem together and make it everyone’s entertainment for the evening, everyone’s game, everyone’s enjoyment.

There is room here for builders, creators, educators, families, gamers, readers, quiet wanderers and cheerful troublemakers. There is room to be with others and room to spend better time with yourself.

04 · The work

What we make

We make storytelling games and creative resources that invite people to play, imagine, reflect and take part.

Our work ranges from family doorway adventures and solo society stories to contested myths and darkly funny dungeon crawls. Each project has its own voice, while sharing the same curiosity about what stories can do.

  • Tabletop roleplaying games
  • Solo and group adventures
  • Journals, prompts and printable aids
  • Creative tools and play resources
05 · The path

How we publish

Mogshade Games is digital first, shaped with the community, and printed when there is demand.

We want to build carefully, openly and sustainably. That means sharing work, listening to the people who gather around it, supporting games after release, and creating physical products when the audience is there to hold them.

The story comes first. The community helps shape the path. The printed thing arrives when the door is ready to stay open.
